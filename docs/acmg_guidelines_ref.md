# ACMG/AMP 2015 Standards Reference

## Overview
Standards and guidelines for the interpretation of sequence variants: a joint consensus recommendation of the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics and the Association for Molecular Pathology (Richards et al., *Genetics in Medicine* 2015).

## Evidence Criteria Taxonomy
- **PVS1**: Null variant (nonsense, frameshift, canonical splice sites) in a gene where loss-of-function is a known disease mechanism.
- **PS1–PS4**: Strong pathogenic evidence (same amino acid change as established pathogenic variant, de novo, functional studies).
- **PM1–PM6**: Moderate pathogenic evidence (mutational hotspot, absence in population databases PM2, novel missense).
- **PP1–PP5**: Supporting pathogenic evidence (co-segregation, in silico predictions, phenotype specificity).
- **BA1**: Standalone benign (allele frequency > 5% in gnomAD/ExAC).
- **BS1–BS4**: Strong benign evidence (allele frequency greater than expected, lack of segregation).
- **BP1–BP7**: Supporting benign evidence (synonymous variant, in silico predictions neutral).
